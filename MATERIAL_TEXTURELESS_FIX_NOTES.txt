BO3 HLSL Previewer - Procedural Custom Material / APE colorMap fix

The custom GBuffer shader remains textureless when the converted HLSL declares no
textures. However, APE/TOOLSGFX still evaluates BO3's auxiliary material editor
contract. Known working BO3 custom-material packages satisfy that contract with a
stock unlit pass whose pixel stage binds colorMap directly to a real editor image.

Current behavior:
- Deferred custom materials use lit_base_shaders + shadowmap_technique_base.
- Only texture/sampler resources actually declared by the custom GBuffer HLSL are
  exposed as custom runtime resources.
- Deferred techsets restore the stock techsetdef_unlit_simple.hlsl auxiliary pass.
- That auxiliary pass declares colorSampler explicitly.
- Runtime auxiliary unlit binds colorMap directly to Image("$white_diffuse").
- TOOLSGFX adds a direct top-level colorMap and also binds the same editor image
  directly inside the unlit ps_generic stage, matching the proven End Portal shape.
- For a procedural material with no albedo, export creates a tiny APE-only white
  editor image with uncompressed / no-mipmap diffuse settings.
- The material GDT colorMap field itself can remain empty; the user's GBuffer shader
  does not gain a colorMap Texture2D dependency.
- The stock debug texel-density pass remains omitted from the custom deferred path.
- Legacy <basename>_internal_white.png files are removed on re-export.

The important distinction is that the APE-only image satisfies editor/auxiliary
material plumbing; it is not sampled by the converted procedural GBuffer shader.
